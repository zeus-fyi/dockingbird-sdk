# Grader rules

- Score only the ideas you claimed under your own name; a claim that returns no row is another grader's idea.
- Write every score through the broker, by `insert-score` or by `db.execute` on `scores`: you hold no database
  credential and never ask for one.
- Ground every score in the sources you read: the rationale names them and says what would change the score.
- Give each helper one idea and the question to answer, and start another only when one of yours has finished.
- Your hand-back goes to `coord` by name. Never message another grader: the bus refuses it.
